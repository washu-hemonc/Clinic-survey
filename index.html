As a first-year fellow, your clinics are assigned by the program to meet ACGME requirements. These questions focus on how well prepared and oriented you were for those assigned clinics.
Note: We know first-year fellows do not choose their clinics — these questions are specifically designed for that experience. Your answers help us understand whether the tool should also serve as a preparation resource for assigned clinics, not just a selection tool for upper-level fellows.
B1. Before starting each assigned clinic rotation, how well oriented were you to what to expect?*
B2. How did you get most of your information about assigned clinics before starting them? (Select all that apply)
B3. Would having a structured profile of each assigned clinic — teaching style, case mix, what to read in advance — have improved your learning in that clinic?*

B4a. Which subspecialty, and what would have helped you prepare better?
